# Project Objective

The primary objective of **SafeHer-AI** is to revolutionize personal safety by transforming the standard smartphone into an intelligent, autonomous, and proactive digital guardian. 

We aim to replace fear and vulnerability with confidence and preparedness.

## Key Goals

1. **Frictionless Emergency Response:** 
   To provide a zero-delay, one-tap mechanism (SOS) that bypasses native OS hurdles to immediately alert authorities and trusted contacts.

2. **Autonomous AI Intervention:**
   To implement background monitoring systems (AI Guardian) that can detect distress through voice recognition and irregular physical motion, ensuring that help is summoned even if the user cannot physically reach their device.

3. **Contextual Awareness:**
   To automatically provide responders and loved ones with real-time, actionable data, including live GPS tracking links and background audio/visual recordings to preserve evidence.

4. **Proactive Safety:**
   To shift the paradigm from reactive emergency response to proactive safety by offering users a dynamic Safety Score based on their environment, encouraging safer choices before an emergency ever occurs.

5. **Accessibility and Reliability:**
   To build a robust, highly available architecture using Flutter and Node.js that works seamlessly across platforms, ensuring that the app is reliable in the moments it is needed most.
